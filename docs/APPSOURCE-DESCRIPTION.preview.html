Most readers want the same starting point every time: this year, their region, the open cases. Chip Slicer opens on the values you choose, even after the reader cleared them last time, and shows every option as a button, with the value of a measure on each chip and a look picked in one click.

Chip Slicer turns a field into a row of buttons — chips — that filter the page with one click. It is free for everyone, with every feature: no Pro tier, no watermark.

✅ WHAT IT ADDS TO A SLICER

  • Default selection: type "2026" or "Spain, France" and the report opens with them selected. If the reader clears it, it stays clear until the page is opened again.
  • Force selection: the slicer always keeps a value selected — your default selection, or else the first value. The "All" chip and the clear button are hidden, so the page never shows everything mixed. Off by default.
  • "+N" for chips that do not fit (horizontal layout with wrapping): in a narrow strip the last chips are grouped into a "+N" chip. One click shows them all, "Show less" closes. Selected chips are never hidden.
  • Clear button (✕) and an optional "All" chip.
  • Counts on the chips: put a measure in Value and each chip shows its count or total, with your model's format.
  • Conditional formatting (fx) on chip backgrounds and text, active and inactive: rules, gradient or field value, chip by chip.
  • Sort by data order, A to Z, Z to A or by value.
  • Horizontal, vertical or grid layout, with automatic or fixed columns, one scrolling row, stretch to fill and alignment. Long labels end with "…".
  • Thirteen style templates. Report theme, the default, takes the colors of your report theme, light or dark, like a native visual. Boardroom, Ledger, Editorial, Signal, Aurora and Terrace are the styles of the TCViz collection, the same looks as in KPI Card and Histogram, so a page that uses them looks consistent. Also Classic, Outline, Soft, Square, Segmented and Dark, plus colors, font, check mark and shadow.
  • Optional header.

🔗 WORKS LIKE A SLICER

  • A standard Power BI filter: it filters the other visuals and works with sync slicers, bookmarks and edit interactions.
  • Single or multi-select; Ctrl+click adds.
  • Text, numbers, dates and hierarchy levels; codes keep their leading zeros.
  • On-object formatting: click a chip or the header to format it on the canvas.
  • Tooltips with the value, the measure and any measures in the Tooltips well; report page tooltips too.
  • Right-click a chip for the Power BI context menu.
  • Chips dim when another visual on the page highlights part of the data.
  • Up to 1,000 values; one field in Category, one optional measure in Value.

♿ ACCESSIBLE AND IN YOUR LANGUAGE

  • Keyboard: one Tab stop, arrow keys, Enter or Space, Escape to clear (unless Force selection is on; with "+N" open, Escape first closes it), Shift+F10 for the context menu. "+N" opens with Enter or Space.
  • Screen readers hear a list with each chip's state.
  • Accessibility card: high contrast (Windows theme, or black on white on demand), focus color and width, and a minimum text size; chips grow so the text fits.
  • 12 languages: English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Japanese, Chinese, Korean.

🔒 PRIVACY

No network calls, no telemetry, no cookies. Your data never leaves Power BI. The links on the start screen open only when you click them.

🚀 GETTING STARTED

  1. Drag a column into Category: one chip per value.
  2. Optional: drag a measure into Value to show counts.
  3. Format pane → Style template: keep Report theme or pick another look in one click.
