WHY THIS UPDATE
- supportUrl in 1.4.0.0 pointed to tcviz.com/support (404) and had no privacy link. Fixed.
- Filter values keep their data type (text codes like 08001 were filtered as numbers; dates and blanks did not filter).
- Filter target read from the column expression (table names with a dot and hierarchy levels failed).
- Keyboard: single Tab stop, arrows, Home/End, Enter/Space, Escape, Shift+F10. role=listbox.
- allowInteractions respected; format pane and landing page localized (en-US, es-ES).

TESTING
1. Open the sample .pbix. The Chip Slicer is on the canvas.
2. Click a chip: other visuals filter. Click it again: filter clears.
3. Format pane > Chip Style > Multi-select on: several chips active; "All" clears.
4. Change page and return: selection is kept. Bookmarks restore it.
5. Change colors, size, gap, layout: applied live.
6. Keyboard: Tab in, arrows to move, Enter/Space to select, Escape to clear, Tab out in one step.
7. Remove the field: landing page appears.
8. High contrast mode: chips stay readable.

PRIVACY AND NETWORK
No network calls, no telemetry, no external libraries loaded at runtime. privileges: []. Only the selected filter is persisted, by Power BI, in the report.
